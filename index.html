What Veribly does

Take a photo in the Veribly camera. The app fingerprints the image (SHA-256) and saves it to your portfolio with a server timestamp. Anyone with your portfolio link can check that each image still matches its fingerprint.

What it proves

  • The image hasn't changed since it was uploaded.
  • The server recorded when it arrived.

What it doesn't prove

  • That the photo was taken on a real device, or that the scene is true. The "live capture" label is set by the browser, so it's self-reported.
  • That the image wasn't edited before upload.

Prototype limits

  • Photos are stored on the server's disk and may be lost if the service is reset.
  • Anyone with your portfolio link can view it.
